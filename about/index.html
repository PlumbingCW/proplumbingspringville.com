Who we are

Springville Pro Plumbing is a plumbing company based at 588 S 2000 W in Springville, Utah. We take emergency calls around the clock and schedule planned work for homes and small businesses in Springville, Mapleton, Spanish Fork, Salem and Provo.

Our approach is simple. We tell you what is wrong in plain language, give you a clear price before work starts and fix the problem. We do not promise arrival times we cannot control, and we do not pad a quote with work you do not need.

What we do

Our work falls into two groups. Emergency and repair work includes drain cleaning, water heater repair, leak detection, burst pipe repair and sewer line repair. Planned work includes water heater installation, repiping and plumbing installation.

Licensing and how to check it

We are licensed and insured. Utah licenses plumbers through the Division of Professional Licensing in apprentice, journeyman and master classes, with residential classes as well. You can verify any plumber, including ours, at secure.utah.gov/llv/search/index.html before you hire.

Ask any plumber who will do the work, whether a permit applies and how inspections will be scheduled. Springville's Building Division at 801-489-2704 can confirm what the city requires for a project.

How a call works

Call the number on this page and describe what you see. The dispatcher asks for your address, the type of problem and anything you have tried. For an emergency, you get steps to limit damage while we dispatch. For planned work, we agree on a time and explain the price before starting.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Principles we work by

We tell you what we find, including when the fix is small. If a clog is a hair plug in a trap, we say so. If a water heater has failed, we say that too and explain your options, including planned alternatives such as tankless service.

We do not use fear to sell work. When a problem can wait for a scheduled visit, we say it can wait. When it cannot, we tell you why and what to do in the meantime.

We keep claims honest. This site does not show review counts, star ratings or years in business, because we would rather show nothing than show a number we cannot back up. What we can show is local facts from public sources, and we cite them.

What you can expect from us

When you call, a person answers. They ask your address, the problem and what you have already tried. You hear what to do first. You hear what we expect the work to involve and the price before we start. When we finish, we test the repair, clean up and tell you what caused the problem and how to avoid it.

If we cannot fix something, we say so. Some jobs need another trade, such as a drywall contractor after a leak or an electrician for a new circuit. We tell you where our work ends and what comes next.
