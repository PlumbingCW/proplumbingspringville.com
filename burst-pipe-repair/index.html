Burst pipe emergency: what to do now

The sequence below takes about two minutes and can save a floor, a ceiling or a finished basement.

Shut off the main water

Turn the main valve clockwise to close it, then open a faucet at the lowest level to drain the lines. This stops the flow and relieves pressure.

Cut power to wet areas

If water is near outlets, appliances or a ceiling fixture, switch off the breaker from a dry spot. Do not stand in water to reach a panel.

Move belongings and photograph

Lift furniture and valuables clear of water. Photograph the damage and the burst pipe before anything is removed, since your insurer will ask.

Call us and keep drains open

Call the 24/7 line. A plumber can arrive with the right repair material while you start drying the area.

How we repair a burst pipe

The repair depends on the pipe material and the break, but the process is consistent.

Confirm and isolate

We locate the break, close the nearest valves and confirm the rest of the system is safe.

Cut out the damaged section

We remove the split or cracked piece and prepare clean pipe ends.

Install the repair

Copper, PEX and CPVC each take a different fitting, solder or coupling, and we match what is already in the house.

Pressure test

We bring the line back up to pressure, check every joint and make sure nothing else is weeping before closing access.

Frozen versus burst: spotting trouble early

A pipe that has frozen but not yet burst can often be saved. Signs include no water or a trickle from a faucet, frost on exposed pipe and a pipe that feels extremely cold. Open the faucet, warm the area gently with a hair dryer or heater placed safely away from water, and never use an open flame.

If water appears or a pipe is bulging, close the main and call. A thaw can reveal a split that only starts leaking as the ice melts, which is why a plumber should check a frozen line even after it flows again.

Springville sits at about 4,554 feet on the west side of the Wasatch Range, with a January mean low near 22°F. Exterior wall plumbing, garage lines, crawl space supply pipes and hose bibs are the usual freeze points, and disconnecting hoses before the first hard freeze removes the easiest one.

Burst pipes also lead to water damage claims. For leaks that have not burst, see our leak detection page, and if the pipe is old and has failed more than once, repiping is worth pricing.
