Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Springville, UT

The main sewer line carries every drain in the house to the city main. When it blocks, sewage can back up into the lowest drain within minutes. Emergency sewer line cleaning in Springville is a 24/7 service from Springville Pro Plumbing, with the right machine sent for the blockage.

Stop using water, including toilets, showers and the washer, and keep people away from any drain that is bubbling or filling. Call and tell us where the water is appearing.

Signs the main sewer line needs cleaning

A blocked main shows up at the lowest points first and then spreads upward.

Basement drain or tub backs up

Gray or dirty water rising in the lowest fixture when you flush upstairs is the classic sign.

Several fixtures drain slowly

When a toilet, tub and sink are all sluggish at once, the main line is restricted.

Gurgling from drains

Air forced through traps by water in the main makes gurgling in unrelated fixtures.

Wet spot in the yard

Wet ground over the sewer path, or a sewage smell outside, can mean overflow at a cleanout or a break.

Sewage backup emergency: protect the house

Raw sewage is a health hazard and can ruin finishes. Move fast.

No water use

Do not flush, shower or run laundry or the dishwasher.

Find the cleanout

The main cleanout is usually a capped pipe near the house or in the basement or crawl space. Do not open it if pressure is behind it.

Wear protection

Boots and gloves protect you from contact. Wash well after any exposure.

How we clean a main sewer line

The method follows the cause. A camera confirms the result.

Access the line

We open the cleanout, or pull a toilet when no cleanout exists, and test the flow.

Cut or jet

A heavy cable machine cuts roots, and a jetter scours grease and scale. We choose based on what the line holds.

Camera confirm

A camera shows the pipe wall and any damage. If the line is damaged, our sewer line repair page lists options.

Plan maintenance

If roots or grease are the cause, we recommend a schedule so the line does not back up again.

Cleanouts and Springville homes

Springville's residential permit checklist includes a sewer lateral inspection for new construction, so the connection to the city main is checked on new builds. Homes without an exterior cleanout may need a toilet pulled to reach the line.

Adding a cleanout is a modest job that makes every future cleaning faster. If yours is buried, locate it before the next problem. Our sewer camera inspection shows the pipe condition from the cleanout.
