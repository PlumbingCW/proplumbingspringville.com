What changes from town to town

Mapleton has large lots and a pressurized irrigation system that covers about half the city, so a spring spike in water use is often a sprinkler problem and not a house leak. Spanish Fork is a bigger system with about 270 miles of main, and the city is responsible for the water line up to the first fitting after the meter.

Salem is small and growing fast, with many newer homes and a separate canal fed irrigation system. Provo is the largest city in our area, with rentals, apartment buildings and businesses, so a backup in one unit can affect others and the property manager should be called too.

Elevation and exposure matter too. Mapleton sits highest on the bench, near 4,748 feet, so cold snaps can hit exposed lines there first. Provo and Springville sit lower, near 4,551 and 4,554 feet, and both have creeks or rivers nearby that rise in spring. Knowing this helps us plan for frozen pipes in winter and wet basements in spring, and it helps you prepare before calling.

In every town, the first steps are the same. Close the main shutoff if water is spreading, stop using drains if anything is backing up and call the 24/7 line. We will tell you what to do while we dispatch.
