Small town, fast growth

Checking for a leak before you call

A leak can hide for weeks. Turn off every tap and appliance, then look at the water meter. If the dial or low flow indicator keeps moving, water is leaking somewhere on your side of the meter. Because Salem's irrigation connections are not individually metered, a moving culinary meter points at the house or the culinary service line rather than the sprinklers.

Check toilets by dropping food coloring in the tank and waiting without flushing. Color in the bowl means a leaking flapper. Look under sinks and around the water heater for damp spots. If you find nothing and the meter still moves, our leak detection service can find the source without tearing into walls.

Freeze season in Salem

Utah winters bring hard freezes. Disconnect hoses, insulate exposed lines and know where your shutoff is before the first cold snap. If a line freezes, close the main, open the nearest tap and call us.

Springville, our base, has a January mean low near 22 degrees Fahrenheit, so plan for hard freezes.
