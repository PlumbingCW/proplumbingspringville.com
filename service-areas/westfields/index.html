What Westfields is

Springville City uses the name Westfields in its own planning. The city's Westfields Community Plan sets a vision for the area, and its Westfields Central New Neighborhood Plan covers about 20 acres next to Meadow Brook Elementary School at roughly 700 South and 950 West. That plan allows up to 220 homes and sets aside about three acres of civic space at the corner of 950 West and 700 South.

The city has not published a street boundary for the community in the documents we reviewed, so we describe it by its anchors. It lies west of 400 West Street and south of the Historic District, and Kelvin Grove Park at 1500 South 1400 West is one of the parks on this side of town.

New and established streets side by side

The central plan was written to connect to the surrounding street grid and to fit the scale of existing homes. That tells us the area is growing around houses that are already there. We have no city figure for how old Westfields homes are, so we do not describe their pipes or fixtures in advance.

What this means for you is simple. A Westfields address could be a recent build with modern fittings or a house that has stood for some time. We ask when the house was built and what you can see before we recommend work.

Why floor height matters on this side of town

Springville's code, in section 10-1-105, covers base floor elevations for all areas west of 400 West Street, which includes the Westfields Overlay Zone. It says the base floor must be no lower than the top back of the curb, with limited exceptions for underground garages and certain commercial buildings. The city lists flood plain rules and geotechnical reports among the conditions for those exceptions.

That is a building rule, not a plumbing rule, and it does not tell us what any one house has. As general plumbing information, how a home's lowest drain sits compared with the sewer main in the street affects how a backup behaves. If you are unsure about yours, a plumber can look at your drains, and the city's Building Division at 801-489-2704 can answer code questions.

First steps at a Westfields address

Do these in order while you reach for the phone.

Close the house supply

The main valve is usually where the line enters the garage, basement or a utility room. A clockwise turn shuts water to every fixture.

Hold the water back

If fixtures gurgle or a floor drain rises, skip laundry, the dishwasher and showers until the line is clear.

Power down wet rooms

Flip the breakers for any room with water on the floor and avoid wet outlets.

Be ready with details

Have the street, the fixtures affected and the year the house went up, if you know it.

Local notes for Westfields

Utah Lake lies roughly three miles west of Springville, so this side of town is the nearest to it. The 2024 city water report gives no hardness number, only dissolved solids, so a simple test of your own tap is the way to decide about a softener. Lawns are watered through the separate pressurized irrigation system, and a wet yard often traces back to a sprinkler line.

Before work that changes pipes, drains or a water heater, ask Springville's Building Division (801-489-2704) whether a permit is needed and how inspections get booked.
