Frozen Pipes in Springville, UT: How to Thaw, Prevent and Repair

If a pipe freezes, open the faucet, find the frozen section and warm it slowly with a hair dryer or warm towels. Never use an open flame, and if a pipe has split or water is spraying, close the main and call a plumber right away.

This guide covers how to thaw a pipe safely, how to prevent freezing in Springville winters and what to do if a pipe bursts.

Pipe frozen or split: what to do first

Act in this order.

Open the nearest faucet

Open the tap on the frozen line. Water flowing as the ice melts relieves pressure and shows when the pipe is clear.

Find the frozen section

It is usually a pipe in an exterior wall, an unheated space or near a vent. It may be frosted or cold to the touch.

Warm it slowly

Use a hair dryer, a heating pad or warm towels. Start near the faucet and work toward the frozen spot. Never use a torch or open flame.

If it splits, close the main

Close the main shutoff and call. A pipe that was split by ice floods when it thaws.

Pipes at risk include those in exterior walls, crawl spaces, garages, attics and any room without heat. Outdoor hose bibs, sprinkler lines and water lines feeding a detached garage freeze first.

How to prevent freezing

Do these before the first deep cold.

Disconnect hoses

Remove hoses from outdoor spigots and shut off interior valves to them, draining the lines.

Insulate exposed pipes

Use foam sleeves on pipes in unheated spaces. Seal air leaks around where pipes pass through walls.

Keep heat on

Keep the thermostat at a steady temperature, even if you travel. Open cabinet doors on exterior walls during hard cold.

Let a tap drip

On extremely cold nights, a slow drip from a vulnerable faucet keeps water moving.

What not to do

Never use a torch, a propane heater or any open flame. Fire and a hidden wall cavity are a dangerous mix. Do not use a space heater near wet floors, and do not leave heaters unattended. Do not pour boiling water on a pipe, since sudden heat can crack it. Avoid electric heat tape unless it is rated for the pipe and used as directed.

If you cannot find the frozen section, or the pipe is behind a finished wall, stop and call. Cutting into a wall without knowing where the pipe is can cause more damage than the freeze.

After thawing: check for damage

A pipe can split without leaking while it is frozen, since ice plugs the crack. Once it thaws, water flows out. After thawing, check the pipe, joints and the wall or ceiling near them for drips or stains. Turn the water on slowly and watch.

If you find a leak, close the main and call. A split in copper, CPVC or PVC cannot be patched permanently, so the section should be replaced. See burst pipe repair for emergency response and leak detection if you suspect a leak you cannot see.

Know your shutoff before you need it

Find the main water shutoff today, not during an emergency. It is usually where the line enters the house, in a basement, garage or utility room. Turn it a few times each year so it does not seize. Label it with a tag so anyone in the house can find it quickly.

Also know where the shutoffs for the water heater, the toilets and the outdoor spigots are. Fixture shutoffs let you stop one leak without shutting off the whole house, which matters when a family needs water while a repair waits.

Warning signs before a freeze

Faucets that run slowly in cold weather, a frosty pipe in a crawl space, or a wall that feels very cold next to a pipe all point to risk. Check the forecast. If several nights in a row will be below freezing, treat it as a freeze event and take the steps above before the cold arrives, not after. Waiting until water stops flowing means the pipe is already frozen, and every hour then raises the chance of a split.

Vacation and vacant homes

A house left empty in winter is the highest risk, because nobody notices a split pipe. Keep the heat on at a safe temperature, ask someone to check the house, and consider shutting off the main and draining the lines if you will be away a long time. Tell your water heater to vacation mode or turn it off as the maker directs.

Hobble Creek and spring thaw

The city reported high spring snowmelt in Hobble Creek in 2023. Freeze and thaw cycles stress pipes and can raise groundwater. After a hard freeze, check your basement and sump as temperatures rise.
