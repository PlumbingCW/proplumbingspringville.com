Tankless Water Heater Service in Springville, UT

Tankless water heaters heat water only when you open a tap, so the tank and its standby heat loss disappear. Tankless water heater service in Springville from Springville Pro Plumbing covers installation, repair and the yearly flush that keeps these units running well.

They are not the right fit for every home. The sections below explain how they work, what they need and when a standard tank is the simpler choice.

How a tankless water heater works

When a hot tap opens, flow sensors fire a burner or energize elements that heat water as it passes through a heat exchanger. When the tap closes, the unit shuts off. There is no stored volume, so you will not run out as long as the unit can keep up with the flow.

The limit is flow rate. If several showers and a dishwasher run at once, a unit sized too small cannot raise the temperature enough. We size the unit to your fixtures and your incoming water temperature.

Tankless or tank: how to decide

Both are reasonable. The right answer depends on the house.

Tankless suits

  • Homes that want endless hot water for normal use
  • Spaces where wall mounting saves floor area
  • Households wanting lower standby heat loss
  • Owners who will schedule yearly flushes

A tank suits

  • Homes with limited gas or electric capacity
  • Lower upfront budget
  • Simple like for like replacement
  • Households with very large simultaneous demand

Is tankless worth it for your household?

The honest answer depends on use. Tankless units save the standby heat a tank loses all day, so households with modest hot water use and good gas capacity tend to benefit most. Households with heavy simultaneous demand may need two units or a larger one.

Installation cost is higher than a like for like tank swap because gas lines, venting and sometimes electrical service change. We give the full picture before you decide, including the yearly flush, so there are no surprises later.

Flushing and descaling

Minerals coat the heat exchanger over time and reduce performance. Most manufacturers call for a yearly flush with a descaling solution, more often where water is hard. The unit needs isolation valves so the flush can run, and we install those on new units.

Springville's city report lists total dissolved solids between 240 and 476 ppm for 2024 but no hardness figure. Test your own tap or ask the Water Division at 801-489-2740, then set your flush schedule accordingly.
