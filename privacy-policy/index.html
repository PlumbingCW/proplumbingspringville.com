Privacy Policy

Overview

This policy explains how Springville Pro Plumbing handles information when you visit this website or call our phone line. We keep it short because we collect very little.

Information you give us

This website has no contact forms, accounts or checkout. If you call us, we may take your name, address, phone number and a description of the problem so we can schedule and perform the work. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use it

We use what you tell us to respond to your request, schedule and perform work, send follow up about that work and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

Website data

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested, for security and reliability. The site embeds Google Maps, which may set its own cookies according to Google's policies.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to do the work, such as with a supplier or inspector, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Email contact@proplumbingspringville.com or call (385) 488-1892.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the bottom shows the latest revision. Last updated November 13, 2026.
