Water Softener and Filtration in Springville, UT

A water softener or filter should be chosen for the water you actually have, not the water a sales brochure assumes. Water softener and filtration service in Springville from Springville Pro Plumbing starts with testing your tap.

Springville City Water System draws from four springs and seven wells. The city's annual report does not list hardness, so we recommend a test before buying anything.

What the city report does and does not say

The 2024 report lists total dissolved solids from 240 to 476 ppm across the system's sources and does not report hardness, calcium or magnesium. It also lists sodium detected between 2.6 and 38 ppm. Total dissolved solids is not hardness, and individual homes can differ depending on the source they receive.

The practical step is to test. A hardness kit from a store gives a number in grains per gallon or parts per million in minutes. You can also call the Water Division at 801-489-2740 to ask how the water is sourced for your street.

Signs of hard water

These are common symptoms. None proves hardness without a test.

White crust on fixtures

Scale on faucets, showerheads and glass is mineral buildup.

Spots on dishes

Cloudy glassware after the dishwasher can come from minerals.

Dry skin and stiff laundry

Soap reacts with minerals and leaves residue.

Short water heater life

Scale on the tank floor and elements shortens appliance life.

Maintaining a softener

A salt based softener needs salt in the brine tank, and the level should be checked every month or two. A salt bridge, a hard crust over the salt, stops the unit from making brine, so a quick poke with a broom handle now and then prevents hard water from returning.

Resin beads wear over many years, and control valves fail like any mechanical part. If hard water returns, check the bypass is not open, the salt is not bridged and the settings still match your water test, then call us.

Lead and flushing: a note from the city report

Springville's report covers lead in home plumbing and recommends flushing taps for 30 seconds to 2 minutes when water has sat for hours before using it for drinking or cooking. It also suggests a filter certified by an ANSI accredited certifier if you want to reduce lead, and the city can discuss testing at 801-489-2740.

Softening does not remove lead, so ask which certification a filter holds. For plumbing that feeds the system, see plumbing installation and our water heater repair page for scale related problems.

Code and permit note

Code note: a softener drain needs an air gap under International Plumbing Code (IPC) principles so waste water cannot flow back into the system.
