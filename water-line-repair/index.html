Emergency Water Line Repair in Springville, UT

The water service line runs from the meter to your house, and when it breaks the first sign is often a soggy patch of lawn, a loss of pressure or a meter that spins with every tap closed. Emergency water line repair in Springville is a 24/7 service from Springville Pro Plumbing.

Who owns which part of the line

Ownership is the first question to settle. In many Utah cities the utility owns the line up to the meter and the homeowner owns the rest. Spanish Fork, for example, states that the city is responsible up to the first fitting after the meter and the owner from there to the building.

Springville's Water Division at 801-489-2740 can tell you where its responsibility ends at your address. Knowing this before a repair avoids calling us for a break that belongs to the city, and it tells you which side needs a permit.

Water line emergency: signs and first steps

A broken service line can erode soil, flood a yard and cut off your water. These signs call for action.

Water surfacing in the yard

Wet ground or a bubbling spot between meter and house, in dry weather, points to a break.

Sudden low pressure

If pressure drops at every fixture without a change in city supply, the line may be leaking or partly collapsed.

Meter spinning with everything off

A moving meter with all fixtures closed means water is escaping somewhere on your side.

No water at all

A full break can drain the line. Call and check the meter box for water pooled inside.

How we repair or replace a water line

A buried line repair needs locating, digging and restoring. We aim to disturb as little as possible.

Locate the line

We confirm the break with a meter and pressure test and trace the pipe route. Utility locate requests are made before excavation.

Open and assess

We expose the break and check the condition of the surrounding pipe to decide between a spot repair and a longer replacement.

Repair or replace

We repair a short break or replace the line with new pipe suited to Utah freeze depth and pressure.

Test and backfill

We pressure test, restore the soil and clean up the yard as agreed.

Pressurized irrigation is a separate system

Springville runs pressurized irrigation as a separate network from the drinking water system. The city asked residents to wait until April 15 in 2024 while it filled the system. Wet ground in spring may come from an irrigation line, and a leak on that system is not a culinary water line.

We separate the two during a visit. If the leak is on your drinking water service line, we repair it. If it is irrigation, we tell you so and refer you to the right trade. For hidden leaks, our leak detection service pinpoints the spot.
