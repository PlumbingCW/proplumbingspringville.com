Disposal emergency: jammed, smoking or leaking

Treat these three as urgent. Each can damage the kitchen or put someone at risk.

Jammed and humming

Switch it off at the wall switch and unplug it or turn off the breaker. Never reach into the chamber. A plumber can free the flywheel safely.

Leaking under the sink

Water at the bottom of the unit often means a failed seal and the unit must be replaced. A leak at the sink flange or drain line can be repaired.

Smoke or burning smell

Cut power at the breaker and do not run it again. A burned motor is not repairable.

Backing up into the sink

If the sink fills when the disposal runs, the clog is likely in the drain line beyond it. Stop and call.

Repair or replace a disposal

Disposals are relatively simple machines, but a failed motor or seal makes replacement the practical choice.

Repair when

  • A jam can be cleared
  • The leak is from a flange or a drain connection
  • The unit is fairly new
  • The motor runs and the grinding is quiet

Replace when

  • The unit leaks from the body
  • The motor is burned out
  • The unit is old and noisy
  • You want a larger horsepower or a quieter model

Double sinks, dishwashers and the right setup

A disposal on a double kitchen sink usually shares a drain line with the second basin and the dishwasher. The dishwasher drain hose connects to the disposal inlet or to an air gap, and the knockout plug in a new disposal must be removed or the dishwasher will not drain.

These details cause many repeat calls after a do it yourself swap. A correct installation also keeps the trap and the vent in good order. Related work like faucet repair and drain cleaning often comes up at the same visit.

What to keep out of the disposal

Grease, coffee grounds in volume, fibrous peels, pasta and rice are the usual causes of slow kitchen lines. Grease solidifies farther down the pipe where the disposal cannot reach, and it builds over months. Cold water while grinding and a few seconds of flushing afterward help.

Springville's municipal code covers what can go into the city's systems, and the Building Division at 801-489-2704 can answer code questions about a kitchen remodel. For anything that already clogged, cable cleaning clears it.
