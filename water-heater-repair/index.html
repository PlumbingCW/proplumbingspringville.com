Water heater emergency: leaking tank or gas smell

A water heater problem becomes an emergency in two ways, water on the floor or gas in the air. The steps are different, so read the one that matches.

Gas smell

Leave the building, do not touch switches and call 911 and your gas utility from outside. Enbridge Gas lists 1-800-767-1689 for gas emergencies in Utah. Call us after the area is safe.

Water on the floor

Close the cold water valve on the line above the tank, then switch off the breaker for an electric unit or turn a gas control to off. A tank that is actively leaking cannot be repaired and usually needs replacement.

Hot water too hot or steaming

Water that scalds or a relief valve that keeps discharging means the tank is overheating or over pressure. Turn the thermostat down, keep people away and call.

No hot water at all

Check the breaker or the pilot window before calling. Many outages are a tripped breaker, a reset button or a pilot that blew out, and the dispatcher can talk you through the checks.

Repair or replace: how we decide

A repair makes sense when the tank is sound and the failed part is inexpensive. Once the tank itself is leaking or the unit is near the end of its life, money spent on parts is money lost.

Repair makes sense when

  • The tank is dry and the shell is not rusted
  • The failed part is a thermostat, element, valve or sensor
  • The unit is within its normal service life
  • The fix is a small share of a new unit's cost

Replace makes sense when

  • Water is leaking from the tank shell
  • The anode rod is gone and rust shows in the hot water
  • Repairs are repeating within a short time
  • The data plate shows the unit is well past its age range

Gas, electric and heat pump units: what fails on each

Gas units fail at the pilot assembly, the thermocouple or flame sensor and the gas control valve. Electric units fail at the elements and thermostats. Both share relief valves, anode rods and dip tubes.

Hybrid heat pump models add a compressor, a fan and a condensate drain to the list, along with air filter and clearance needs. If yours shows an error code, write it down before calling because it narrows the fault quickly. When a repair is not worth it, our water heater replacement page covers the next step, and tankless service covers on-demand units.

Springville water and your tank

Springville City Water System draws from four springs and seven wells, and its annual report lists total dissolved solids between 240 and 476 ppm for 2024. The report does not give a hardness figure, so the honest advice is to test your own tap with a store hardness kit or ask the Water Division at 801-489-2740.

That matters because minerals settle as sediment on the tank floor and on electric elements. A tank flushed on a regular schedule and an anode rod checked every few years last longer than one nobody touches, whatever the water turns out to be.
