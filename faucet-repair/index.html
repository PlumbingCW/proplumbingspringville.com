When a faucet problem is an emergency

A faucet that sprays, a handle that came off in your hand or a supply line that burst under the sink can flood a cabinet in minutes. Close the valve under the sink first, then the main if needed. Photograph the area and call.

Dripping and weeping are less urgent but not free. A faucet leaking one drip a second can waste thousands of gallons a year, and a leak under a sink can rot a cabinet floor before anyone sees it.

Faucet types and how they fail

The internal design tells a plumber what to bring. Knowing yours helps when you call.

How we repair a faucet

The work is simple in principle, but old fixtures hide corroded fittings that turn a ten minute fix into a longer one.

Shut off and open

We close the supply valves, remove the handle and trim and inspect the cartridge or washer.

Replace worn parts

We install matching parts, or replace the faucet if the body is corroded or parts are no longer available.

Check the shutoffs

Supply valves under old sinks often seize. We test them and replace any that do not close fully.

Test for leaks

We run hot and cold, check the drain and supply connections and clean up before leaving.

Outdoor spigots and winter

Hose bibs are a common emergency in cold weather. Springville sees January lows around 22°F, and a hose left connected can trap water that freezes and splits the pipe behind the wall. You may not know until spring when water pours inside.

Disconnect hoses before the first hard freeze and shut off any interior valve that feeds an outdoor spigot. If a spigot already drips from inside the wall, call, because that may be a freeze split behind the siding, and our burst pipe repair team can fix it.

Know your shutoffs before you need them

Every fixture should have its own shutoff valve, and each should turn. Test yours once a year and replace any that are stuck. The city's Water Division at 801-489-2740 can help locate your main shutoff at the meter if you cannot find it inside the house.

If a sink drain is slow while the faucet is fixed, see our garbage disposal page when a disposal is involved, or drain cleaning for the sink itself.
