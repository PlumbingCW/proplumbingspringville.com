Basement Waterproofing in Springville, UT

Basement water has a cause, and waterproofing starts by finding it. Basement waterproofing in Springville from Springville Pro Plumbing focuses on the plumbing side: sump pits, pumps, discharge lines and interior drainage.

We do not sell sealants as a cure for flooding. If water is entering from the ground, the answer is usually to give it somewhere to go.

Where basement water comes from

Water enters through cracks in walls and floors, at the joint between wall and slab, around windows and window wells, through failing pipes and, in wet years, up through the slab from groundwater. The cause decides the fix.

A roof or grading problem is cheaper to fix than a drain system, so we check those first. If water is rising from the ground, a sump pump is the standard answer, and our sump pump page covers it.

Springville discharge rules

Springville's code says a sump needs city approval, and discharges from foundation drains, footing drains and basement pumps need written approval. Where you send the water matters, so call the Building Division at 801-489-2704 before routing a discharge.

The city also reminds owners that Hobble Creek channels can clog with debris and that creek bank debris is the owner's responsibility. Sending water toward a clogged channel is a poor plan.

Steps you can take today

Check that gutters are clear and downspouts discharge several feet from the foundation. Make sure soil slopes away from the house. Look at window wells for debris and standing water. Test your sump pump by pouring water into the pit until the float lifts, and confirm the discharge runs outside.

Move valuables off the floor in areas that have taken water before. If water is already entering, shut off power to wet areas before touching anything and call us. These steps cost little and often solve part of the problem.

Ground, creek and spring

Springville sits roughly three miles east of Utah Lake with Hobble Creek running through town and spring snowmelt from the Wasatch. The city noted high spring flows in Hobble Creek in 2023. A basement that stays dry in August may take water in April.

Ask neighbors, but do not assume what one house sees applies to another. For emergencies, see burst pipe repair when the water comes from a pipe, and note that a failed sump is an emergency covered on the sump page.

Code and permit note

Code note: a sump system is local first and code second. Springville approves sumps, and the International Plumbing Code (IPC) guides the plumbing around them.
